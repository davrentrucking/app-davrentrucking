OUR CAPABILITIES

Specialized Fleet Solutions

Choose the right transport service tailored to your cargo requirements.

Temperature Controlled
01

Reefer Trucking

Precision temperature management for perishable, pharmaceutical, and sensitive freight across Canada.

Our state-of-the-art refrigerated trailers maintain continuous, monitored temperatures from origin to destination. Whether you're shipping frozen food, fresh produce, dairy, or pharmaceuticals, our drivers follow strict cold-chain protocols to protect your product integrity.

  • Multi-temperature zone capability
  • 24/7 continuous temperature data logging
  • Pre-cooling protocols before loading
  • HACCP and food safety compliant

Heavy & Oversized
02

Flatbed Transport

Versatile, heavy-haul solutions for construction materials, machinery, steel, and oversized cargo.

When freight can't fit in a standard enclosed trailer, our flatbed division provides the flexibility, secure strapping, and proper tarping required. Our drivers are trained in load securement regulations to ensure your cargo arrives safely and in perfect condition.

  • Heavy-haul and oversized permit routing
  • Professional tarping and weatherproofing
  • Side-load and crane-load accessibility
  • Construction and industrial site delivery
Get Flatbed Quote
HAZMAT Certified
03

Hazardous Shipping

Certified, compliant, and strictly managed transportation for dangerous goods and chemical cargo.

Davren Trucking is fully certified under TDG (Transportation of Dangerous Goods) regulations. Our drivers receive rigorous hazardous materials training, our equipment is placarded and inspected, and every load is routed in strict accordance with federal and provincial guidelines.

  • TDG & HAZMAT certified drivers
  • Full compliance documentation and placarding
  • Spill-kit equipped and safety emergency readiness
  • Regulated route planning and permit handling
