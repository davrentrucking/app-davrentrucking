WHO WE ARE

Canada's Trusted Trucking Partner

Davren Trucking Ltd. is a full-service transportation and logistics company proudly serving businesses across Canada and internationally. Founded on the principles of safety, reliability, and integrity, we have grown into a trusted partner for businesses of all sizes.

Our modern fleet and experienced team are dedicated to delivering your freight on time, every time — whether it's a temperature-controlled reefer load, an oversized flatbed shipment, or regulated hazardous materials.
