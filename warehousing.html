OUR SERVICES

Warehousing Solutions

From short-term storage to full third-party logistics, we have the infrastructure and expertise to handle it all.

General Warehousing

Clean, secure, and climate-monitored storage for a wide range of general freight and consumer goods. Short and long-term options available with flexible lease arrangements.

  • Pallet & bulk storage
  • Flexible short & long-term terms
  • 24/7 security & CCTV monitoring

Cold Storage

Temperature-controlled storage facilities for perishable goods, pharmaceuticals, and any product that requires strict environmental conditions throughout its storage life.

  • -20°C to +10°C temperature zones
  • Continuous temperature monitoring
  • HACCP-compliant procedures

Cross-Docking

Minimize storage time and costs by transferring inbound shipments directly to outbound vehicles. Our cross-docking service keeps your supply chain lean and fast.

  • Same-day transfer capability
  • Reduced handling & storage costs
  • Coordinated inbound/outbound scheduling

Distribution & Fulfillment

End-to-end order fulfillment services including receiving, picking, packing, labeling, and outbound shipping — fully integrated with your inventory management system.

  • Pick, pack & ship operations
  • Returns management
  • WMS integration available

Hazmat Storage

Licensed and compliant storage for dangerous goods and regulated materials. Full TDG documentation, segregation protocols, and emergency response plans in place.

  • TDG & WHMIS compliant
  • Segregated storage zones
  • Emergency response on standby

Inventory Management

Real-time inventory visibility through our warehouse management system. Know exactly what you have, where it is, and when it moves — at any time, from anywhere.

  • Real-time stock visibility
  • Barcode & RFID tracking
  • Custom reporting & alerts

HOW IT WORKS

Your Freight, Our Responsibility

A straightforward, transparent process from the moment your goods arrive to the day they ship out.

01

Inbound Receiving

Freight arrives at our dock and is inspected, counted, and logged into our WMS with full condition documentation.

02

Secure Storage

Your goods are assigned a dedicated storage location — racked, floor, cold, or hazmat zone depending on requirements.

03

Pick & Pack

Orders are picked, packed to spec, labeled, and staged for outbound shipment — accurately and on schedule.

04

Outbound Dispatch

Shipments are dispatched via Davren's own fleet or your preferred carrier, with full tracking and documentation.
